# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, beliefs, experience, or other personal characteristics.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Communicating respectfully with other contributors
* Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
* Keeping discussions relevant to the project and working toward constructive outcomes
* Showing consideration toward other community members

Disagreement is a normal part of open collaboration. Contributors are expected to express disagreement respectfully. Disagreement with an idea or viewpoint, when expressed without harassment or personal abuse, is not by itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention or advances
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
* Harassment, threats, or intimidation
* Publishing another person's private information without their explicit permission
* Repeated unwanted personal contact after a clear request to stop
* Targeting another contributor through harassment or personal abuse because of their background, identity, beliefs, or other personal characteristics
* Other conduct that a reasonable person would understand as harassment, intimidation, threats, or deliberate personal abuse that materially interferes with another person's ability to participate in the project

Criticism of code, designs, proposals, technical decisions, or other contributions is not a personal attack when it is directed at the work rather than the person and is expressed in a manner consistent with this Code of Conduct.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, documentation, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also restrict or suspend participation, temporarily or permanently, when warranted by the severity, context, or pattern of a contributor's conduct.

Enforcement decisions must be based on conduct governed by this Code of Conduct, not on whether maintainers agree or disagree with a contributor's personal, political, religious, philosophical, or social views.

Where reasonably possible, a maintainer with a significant personal conflict involving a report should not be solely responsible for deciding its outcome.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces
when an individual is representing the project or its community. Examples of
representing a project or community include using an official project e-mail
address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed
representative at an online or offline event. Representation of a project may be
further defined and clarified by project maintainers.

## Enforcement

Instances of harassment, threats, abuse, or other conduct that may violate this Code of Conduct may be reported by contacting the project team at coc@linuxcontainers.org.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and with appropriate consideration of the surrounding context. Responses should be fair and proportionate to the nature, severity, and pattern of the conduct involved.

The project team will protect the confidentiality of reporters and other involved parties to the extent reasonably possible while investigating and resolving a report.


Additional enforcement procedures may be documented separately by project leadership.

Project maintainers who intentionally disregard this Code of Conduct, enforce it in bad faith, or abuse their enforcement authority may themselves be subject to corrective action by project leadership.
